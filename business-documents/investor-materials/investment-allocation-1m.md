# $1M Investment Allocation Plan for AI Hive

*Date: January 2025*
*Investment Amount: $1,000,000*
*Runway Target: 18 months*

## Executive Summary

This document outlines the strategic allocation of a $1M seed investment for AI Hive, focusing on three key priorities:
1. **SaaS Platform Development** (25.2%) - Transform proven technology into scalable SaaS
2. **Sales & Marketing** (40%) - Direct sales to startups and entrepreneurs at tech events
3. **Operations & Infrastructure** (34.8%) - Team, working capital, and business operations

The plan provides 18 months of runway to achieve product-market fit and reach $250K MRR with 60%+ gross margins. AI costs of $50/day per customer are fully covered by customer payments - the investment covers working capital for cash flow timing, not operational AI costs. The platform will transition from human DevOps support (months 1-9) to full self-management by AI Hive itself (months 10+).
